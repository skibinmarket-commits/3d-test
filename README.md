# 3D-printable SUP cup and phone holder

`Model4_SUP_phone_cup_holder.stl` is a one-piece holder designed to sit under
the elastic deck cords of a SUP board. GitHub can display the STL directly in
the browser.

## Model 4 dimensions

- Cup: 76 mm inside diameter, 82 mm outside diameter, 100 mm usable height.
- Cup wall: 3 mm; four 7 mm vertical slots spaced 90 degrees apart.
- Round cup platform: 110 mm diameter and 5 mm thick.
- Cup floor above the platform: 3 mm.
- Phone pocket: 81 x 16 x 80 mm internal space.
- Phone walls: 3 mm; phone floor: 5 mm.
- Six 4 mm drain holes with softened upper edges.
- Rounded platform corners and reinforced cup-to-phone joints.

The editable OpenSCAD source is `Model4_SUP_phone_cup_holder.scad`. The checked
STL is generated by `generate_sup_holder.py` using `trimesh` and `manifold3d`.

## PLA+ printing starting point

- Orient flat on the platform; supports should not be needed.
- 0.20 mm layer height, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers.
- 25-35% gyroid infill.
- Use a brim if bed adhesion is marginal.
- PLA+ is usable for testing, but do not leave the part in a hot car or prolonged
  direct sun; inspect it before every trip because SUP deck cords can load it.
