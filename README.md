# 3D-printable SUP cup and phone holder

## 60 mm branch scoop

`Лопатка Model 1.stl` is a separate miniature scoop based on the supplied
concept image. It is exactly 60 mm long, approximately 16 mm wide, and has a
2 mm bowl floor, printable branch details, and a flat back for support-free
printing. Place the flat back on the build plate with the cavity facing upward.

Suggested PLA+ starting point: 0.16-0.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 15-25% infill,
supports off, and a 3-5 mm brim if bed adhesion is uncertain.

The editable source is `Лопатка Model 1.scad`; the checked STL is generated by
`generate_branch_scoop.py`.

## SUP holder

`Model4_SUP_phone_cup_holder.stl` is a one-piece holder designed to sit under
the elastic deck cords of a SUP board. GitHub can display the STL directly in
the browser.

## Model 4 dimensions

- Cup: 76 mm inside diameter, 82 mm outside diameter, 100 mm usable height.
- Cup wall: 3 mm; four 7 mm vertical slots spaced 90 degrees apart.
- Round cup platform: 110 mm diameter and 5 mm thick.
- Cup floor above the platform: 3 mm.
- Phone pocket: 91 x 16 x 80 mm internal space.
- Phone walls: 3 mm; phone floor: 5 mm.
- Six 4 mm drain holes, each fed by a shallow asymmetric rounded-triangle
  collector inspired by a guitar-pick shape.
- R3 rounding on the exposed upper slot corners.
- External circular reinforcement with a 45-degree slope at the cup base; its
  hollow centre preserves the clear cup bore and drainage.
- 10 mm bold `Red Rocket` engraving, approximately 1 mm deep.
- Rounded platform corners and reinforced cup-to-phone joints.
- The final cup bore is re-cut to a clear 76 mm diameter with no internal ribs.
- Four 7 mm vertical rope channels are open at the top and terminate in rounded
  U-shaped seats approximately 2 mm below the former slot ending. Each channel
  continues as an 8 mm wide, approximately 2.5 mm deep open groove which follows
  the sloped reinforcement surface and then exits across the platform edge. The
  runoff is subtracted after assembly, so it passes through the cup wall and the
  reinforcement rather than remaining trapped inside the cup. Overlapping round
  transitions keep the channel floor continuous, without transverse lips. A
  separate vertical opening removes any roof above the rounded floor, keeping
  the entire route an open U-groove rather than a tunnel. The complete slot and
  runoff are united into one final smooth cutting tool and re-cut through the
  assembled model, removing residual material at every component boundary. The
  two channel walls are lofted as continuous surfaces at a constant 7 mm width,
  eliminating width changes and stepped intersections along the route.

The editable OpenSCAD source is `Model4_SUP_phone_cup_holder.scad`. The checked
STL is generated by `generate_sup_holder.py` using `trimesh` and `manifold3d`.

## PLA+ printing starting point

- Orient flat on the platform; supports should not be needed.
- 0.20 mm layer height, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers.
- 25-35% gyroid infill.
- Use a brim if bed adhesion is marginal.
- PLA+ is usable for testing, but do not leave the part in a hot car or prolonged
  direct sun; inspect it before every trip because SUP deck cords can load it.
